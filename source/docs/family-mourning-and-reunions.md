# Family mourning and reunions

A newly recorded death locks living family members into Depressed for 120,000 world ticks (five Minecraft days). The timer is saved in the family tree and elapses while relatives are unloaded. Gifts and other mood adjustments cannot override it. A widowed person cannot marry, become engaged, or be promised to a new partner during this period. Ring gifts, matchmaking, automatic village marriages and player proposals respect this restriction.

Revival clears grief for the returned relative immediately and starts a five-day Upbeat lock for the returned person and surviving relatives. At their next safe nearby meeting, the relatives face one another, both say they missed each other, and use a three-second embrace pose. Reunions wait while either person is fighting, sleeping, mounted or not in the same loaded area. Solid walls prevent hugging. Pending reunions and timers persist in the family tree. A later death starts a fresh mourning period; duplicate revival completion does not extend the same celebration indefinitely. Normal mood changes become available again when the timer expires. Existing marriage restoration avoids replacing a new spouse if a survivor has remarried after mourning ends.

Redemption now clears a raider's old workplace, combat, navigation and movement order state after changing profession. Both pardon and settlement use this cleanup, including witches. Befriending a hostile raider remains distinct from choosing redemption. Vindicators must also be within a short horizontal melee distance of the target's hitbox, as well as passing the existing vanilla range and fresh obstruction checks.

Validation: `python3 source/tools/validate_family_emotions.py` (38 production-method fixture checks) and `python3 source/tools/validate_raider_reports.py` (15 checks). All six Fabric/Quilt and NeoForge release builds passed. No Minecraft client or server was launched, so gameplay and the embrace animation have not been checked in-game. No Spanish support or Cinematic Interactions change was added.

Reunion speech reserves argument 1 for the listening player and argument 2 for the relative; the reunion line uses argument 2. Hugs are exclusive pairs, start within 0.9 blocks, maintain both body/head directions every tick after ordinary AI, and clear reciprocal pending events to avoid repeating the same hug. Other relatives wait their turn. A new combat target or separation interrupts the embrace.
